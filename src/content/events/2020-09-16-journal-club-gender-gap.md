Speaker: Rachael Alfant

The National Mathematics Survey, conducted by Amanda Glazer, examined the undergraduate gender gap in mathematics at Harvard, MIT, Yale, Princeton, and Brown. The session discussed possible causes of the larger gap at those institutions and approaches for closing it.
