Speaker: Jonas Actor

Readings for the session, each with a different emphasis for the CAAM community:

- Problem statement and variants: X. Wang and Y.J. Zhang, “Nonnegative matrix factorization: a comprehensive review,” _IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering_, 2013.
- Algorithms: C.J. Lin, “Projected gradient methods for nonnegative matrix factorization,” _Neural Computation_, 2007. This paper was communicated to the journal editor by Yin Zhang and cites Tapia and McCormick (1972).
- Active set methods and linear programming: H. Kim and H. Park, “Nonnegative matrix factorization based on alternating nonnegativity constrained least squares and active set method,” _SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis and Applications_, 2008.
- Constrained optimization: M. Teboulle and Y. Vaisbourd, “Novel proximal gradient methods for nonnegative matrix factorization with sparsity constraint,” _SIAM Journal on Imaging Sciences_, 2020.
- Graphs and clustering: D. Kuang, C. Ding, and H. Park, “Symmetric nonnegative matrix factorization for graph clustering,” _Proceedings of the 2012 SIAM International Conference on Data Mining_, 2012.
- Computational complexity: S. Vavasis, “On the complexity of nonnegative matrix factorization,” _SIAM Journal on Optimization_, 2009.
