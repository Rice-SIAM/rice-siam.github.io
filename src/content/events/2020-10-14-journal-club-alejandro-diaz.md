Speaker: Alejandro Diaz

Many science and engineering problems require simulating large-scale dynamical systems. Model reduction extracts the relevant dynamics so that a smaller system can approximate the original input-to-output behavior. This talk derived the Loewner framework, related it to earlier model reduction methods, and presented numerical examples.
