Speaker: Raul Garcia

Discussion of Owen, J.H. and Mehrotra, S. (2001), “On the value of binary expansions for general mixed-integer linear programs.” Methods that use variable disjunctions to generate cuts can be powerful for mixed binary linear programs, but remodeling a mixed-integer linear program as a mixed binary problem can make the resulting formulation more expensive to solve.
