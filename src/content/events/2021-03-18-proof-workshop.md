Presenters: Rachael Alfant (CAAM) and Bilyana Tzolova (CAAM).

The workshop covered induction, writing a basic proof, proof by contradiction, and related techniques that appear in core mathematics, computational and applied mathematics, and statistics courses after calculus. Co-sponsored by Rice SIAM and Rice AWM.
