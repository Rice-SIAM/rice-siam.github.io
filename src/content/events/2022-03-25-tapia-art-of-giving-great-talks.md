Speaker: University Professor Richard Tapia

Giving excellent talks promotes professional success. This lecture covered what to do and what not to do when speaking, with examples from the speaker’s experience. Co-sponsored by Rice SIAM, Rice AWM, and Rice Graduate Education for Minorities (RGEM). A reception followed, with music by Rice’s mariachi.
