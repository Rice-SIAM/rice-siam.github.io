Speaker: Nicola Sassone

Talk: Optimal drift control of a two-sided reflected Brownian motion under an ergodic risk-sensitive cost criterion

We consider the ergodic risk-sensitive drift control problem for a two sided reflected Brownian motion, whose objective consists of a running cost that is a function of the drift, and a rejection cost that is determined by the upper reflection process. We first solve this problem explicitly by constructing a solution pair to the multiplicative Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman equation, which we then use to construct an optimal stationary Markov policy. Next, we characterize the effect of the risk sensitivity parameter on the optimal cost, the value function and the optimal policy as well as the long-run average rejection rate, in particular, the associated monotonicity properties and their convergence to the corresponding quantities in the risk-neutral setting as the sensitivity parameter goes to zero. Finally, we show that solving the risk-sensitive drift control problem is equivalent to solving a certain long-run average cost two-person zero-sum stochastic differential game for an extended diffusion with two-sided reflections.

This is a joint work with Dr. Guodong Pang.
