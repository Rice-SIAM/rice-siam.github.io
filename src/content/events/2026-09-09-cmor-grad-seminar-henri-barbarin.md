Speaker: Henri Barbarin, Office of Research Security & Export Controls

This session reviews key research security risks researchers face and the warning signs to watch for. It covers data and export control compliance, malign foreign talent recruitment programs and common indicators of concern, and recommendations and guidance for international travel.
