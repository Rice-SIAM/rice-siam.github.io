## Who is welcome

Rice SIAM is for Rice University students interested in applied mathematics, computational science, scientific computing, and related fields. Undergraduate and graduate students are welcome, including students outside CMOR.

## How to take part

- Come to [chapter events](/events), or [add them to your calendar](/events#calendar).
- Attend [workshops and SIAM meetings](/conferences).
- Look at [internships, postdocs, fellowships, and jobs](/opportunities).
- If you want to help organize events or serve as an officer, [contact the chapter](/contact).

## SIAM student membership

Chapter events do not require SIAM membership. SIAM, the professional society, offers its own student membership, which can be free or discounted. Rice University is on [SIAM’s academic member list](https://epubs.siam.org/academic-members). [SIAM’s student membership page](https://www.siam.org/membership/individual-membership/student-membership/) says master’s and Ph.D. students at an academic member institution can join SIAM for free. Eligibility and how to apply are on that page.
