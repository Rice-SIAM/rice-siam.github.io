## Who is welcome

Rice SIAM is for Rice University students interested in applied mathematics, computational science, scientific computing, and related fields. Undergraduate and graduate students are welcome, including students outside CMOR.

## How to take part

- Watch the [events page](/events) and show up.
- See internships, postdocs, and jobs on the [opportunities page](/opportunities).
- If you want to help organize events or serve as an officer, use the [contact page](/contact).

## SIAM student membership

Chapter events do not require SIAM membership. SIAM, the professional society, offers its own student membership, which can be free or discounted. Eligibility and how to apply are on SIAM’s [student membership page](https://www.siam.org/membership/individual-membership/student-membership/).
